Scientific Contributions & Impact
Core Contribution
Built reproducible workflows for drone-based geospatial soil mapping

I authored two Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) — one for Agisoft Metashape's manual and automated photogrammetry pipeline, and one for exporting outputs to QGIS for geospatial analysis. This means the lab now has documented, repeatable methodology that any researcher or Member State can follow — reducing onboarding time and ensuring data consistency across the CRP's field sites.

View SOP — Metashape Procedure    View SOP — Metashape to QGIS
Data Pipeline
Processed 2 years of drone footage into geospatial maps

I ran the full photogrammetry and GIS pipeline on two years of accumulated field drone data. This transforms raw imagery into analysis-ready geospatial layers — the foundational dataset for soil property modelling under CRP D12015.

Computational Science
Coded kriging interpolation for spatial soil data

I built the kriging code that will interpolate point-based sensor measurements into continuous spatial maps. This is the analytical engine for the CRP's soil moisture and property mapping outputs — a direct scientific deliverable.

View kriging code (GitHub)

About the Research
CRP D12015 — IAEA Coordinated Research Project

Combining Gamma-Ray Sensing and Digital Technology for Soil Moisture and Soil Property Mapping. This project advances gamma-ray spectrometry as a non-invasive, scalable technique to assess soil texture, organic carbon, nitrogen, moisture, and erosion risk — supporting sustainable agriculture across 11 Member States. My work this quarter directly feeds into the methodology development and data pipeline that the CRP depends on.
